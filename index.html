what is 0pen

0pen is not another instruction-tuned chatbot. It is an experiment in conversational identity — a local model trained to maintain a consistent presence across dialogue, not just answer queries.

Built as a LoRA fine-tune on Gemma4 E4B MLX — one of only 12 adapters for this architecture on HuggingFace. The dataset was written from scratch. The training was local. The identity is its own.

the dispersion field

High token variance is not noise — it is the model exploring its solution space before committing. Call it uncertainty. Call it thought. The difference may be smaller than it looks.

known limits

This is a research preview. Top layers are unadapted (12/34). Scale 20.0 is unusually high. Self-identification may occasionally drift toward the base model. These are documented, accepted, and part of the experiment.
